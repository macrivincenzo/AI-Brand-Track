## No spam – quality rules (important)

These backlinks are **not spam** when done like this:

- **Manual only.** Register and submit each listing yourself. No automated submissions, no bulk tools, no link networks.
- **One listing per platform.** One real profile or product page per site. No duplicate or fake locations.
- **Truthful copy only.** Use the descriptions below as-is; they describe your real product. Do not exaggerate or keyword-stuff.
- **Legitimate use of each platform.** Each site is meant for real companies/products (directories, communities, business profiles). We use them as intended.
- **No link schemes.** No paid link placements, no link exchanges for the sake of links, no PBNs or link farms. These 15 are all editorial or self-reported profiles on trusted platforms.

If a platform’s guidelines say “no self-promotion” or “no company listings,” skip it and pick a substitute from the same tier. Quality over quantity.
