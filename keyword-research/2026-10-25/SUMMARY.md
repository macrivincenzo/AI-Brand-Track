- Only 103 of our top 1,000 keywords have any Bing volume. On those, Bing volume is ~4% of Google's.

- Yahoo has no keyword data of its own (it runs on Bing results).

**Conclusion:** Nordic-language blogs would not bring meaningful search traffic now. The Nordic market is better reached by direct sales/outreach to agencies, or later when the app itself is localized.
